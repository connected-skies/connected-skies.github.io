About the workshop

Drones at the frontier of cyber‑physical systems

Drones are no longer isolated flying machines: they are connected nodes in networks of sensors, vehicles, infrastructure and cloud services. This openness enables new applications — and exposes new attack surfaces.

Connected Skies brings together researchers from the Department of Computer Science of the University of Pisa and KAUST to discuss how to make connected cyber-physical systems secure by design: from the communication paradigms that tie them together, to the threats that target them, to AI-based techniques that can detect and defend against attacks in real time.

Themes

Paradigms, Threats and AI‑based Defenses

01

Paradigms

Architectures and communication models for connected cyber-physical systems: drone swarms, edge–cloud continuum, UAV-assisted networks and the protocols that keep them coordinated.

02

Threats

Jamming, spoofing, hijacking and data poisoning: understanding the attack surface of systems where a cyber attack can have direct physical consequences.

03

AI‑based Defenses

Machine learning for intrusion and anomaly detection, resilient control and trustworthy autonomy — and the security of the AI models themselves.
